SHOP HANI — LOGO PACKAGE
========================

The mark: a rounded shopping bag with a smile cut out of it and an orange
handle. The wordmark is set in Outfit (Latin, "Shop Hani") and Alexandria
(Arabic, شوپ هاني), converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed.
"Shop" / "شوپ" is set in ink and "Hani" / "هاني" in brand blue.

FOLDERS
  svg/            Vector masters. Use these for print, signage, packaging, Canva.
  png/logo/       Lockups at 64, 128, 256 and 512 px tall (transparent background).
  png/mark/       The bag symbol alone, 16 to 1024 px (transparent).
  png/app-icon/   Square icons with background, 180 to 1024 px.
                  icon-ios = Apple / full-bleed, icon-maskable = Android adaptive.
  favicon/        favicon.ico (16/32/48), favicon.svg and PNG sizes.
  social/         1200x630 share image (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram links).

VARIANTS
  color       for light backgrounds (white, light grey)
  dark        for dark backgrounds (ink, black, photos)
  mono-black  one colour, for stamps, receipts, single-colour print
  mono-white  one colour, reversed out of any brand colour or photo

LOCKUPS
  horizontal  mark + "Shop Hani"         (website header, invoices, email)
  stacked     mark + Latin + Arabic      (packaging, stickers, Instagram profile)
  arabic      mark + شوپ هاني            (Arabic materials; mark sits on the right)

COLOURS
  Ink     #111827   text, dark backgrounds
  Blue    #2B50EE   the bag and "Hani"
  Orange  #FF6A3D   handle, sale tags, call-to-action accents
  Yellow  #FFC233   highlights
  Green   #16A34A   "in stock", success
  Light   #F5F6FA   page background

RULES
  Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bag handle.
  Smallest size: 24 px tall on screen (mark alone: 16 px), 10 mm in print.
  Don't stretch, rotate, recolour outside the variants, or add shadows.
