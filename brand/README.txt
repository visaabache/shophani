SHOPHANI — LOGO PACKAGE
=======================

The mark: a shopping bag whose window is a Moroccan horseshoe arch, holding the
eight-pointed zellige star. The wordmark is set in Fraunces Bold (Latin) and
Reem Kufi Bold (Arabic, شوبهاني), converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed.
The dot of the "i" is a small gold zellige star.

FOLDERS
  svg/            Vector masters. Use these for print, signage, packaging, Canva.
  png/logo/       Lockups at 64, 128, 256 and 512 px tall (transparent background).
  png/mark/       The bag symbol alone, 16 to 1024 px (transparent).
  png/app-icon/   Square icons with background, 180 to 1024 px.
                  icon-ios = Apple / full-bleed, icon-maskable = Android adaptive.
  favicon/        favicon.ico (16/32/48), favicon.svg and PNG sizes.
  social/         1200x630 share image (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram links).
  brand-overview.png  All variants on one sheet.

VARIANTS
  color       for light backgrounds (cream, white, sand)
  dark        for dark backgrounds (ink, black, photos)
  mono-black  one colour, for stamps, receipts, embroidery, single-colour print
  mono-white  one colour, reversed out of any brand colour or photo

LOCKUPS
  horizontal  mark + "shophani"      (website header, invoices, email)
  stacked     mark + Latin + Arabic  (packaging, stickers, Instagram profile)
  arabic      mark + شوبهاني         (Arabic materials; mark sits on the right)

COLOURS
  Ink        #16142B   text, dark backgrounds
  Majorelle  #2747C9   the bag (on dark backgrounds use #4F6CF0)
  Gold       #E3A93A   handle and star
  Terracotta #C4553A   accent (Arabic name in the stacked colour lockup)
  Cream      #FFFAF2   light background

RULES
  Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bag handle.
  Smallest size: 24 px tall on screen (mark alone: 16 px), 10 mm in print.
  Don't stretch, rotate, recolour outside the variants, or add shadows.

Fonts (SIL Open Font License): Fraunces by Undercase Type, Reem Kufi by Khaled Hosny.
